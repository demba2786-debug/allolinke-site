Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Allo Linke is an app to send mobile airtime and data bundles, pay for electricity and call Africa and the rest of the world. This page explains what data we collect, why we use it, who we share it with and what your rights are under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Who we are

The data controller is Allo Linke, Italy. Contact: contact@allolinke.com. Full company details (legal name, address, VAT number) will be added here once the company is registered.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: phone number and, if you add them, name and email address.
  • Sign in with Google: if you choose “Continue with Google”, we only receive your name, email address and Google profile picture. We never have access to your Google password.
  • ID document (if requested): a photo sent for identity verification, to prevent fraud.
  • Recipients: numbers and names of the people you top up or call.
  • Transactions: history of top-ups, calls, subscriptions, electricity payments and codes used.
  • Support: messages exchanged with customer support.
  • Partner shops: shop name and sales log.

We do not collect your location or any advertising identifier. The camera is only used to scan top-up cards and to photograph your ID document. Access to contacts, if you allow it, is only used to pick a number to top up faster; your contacts are not sent to our servers.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We use your data to provide the services you buy (performance of a contract), protect your account and prevent fraud (legitimate interest), answer support requests and comply with legal and accounting obligations (legal obligation). We do not sell your data and we do not run targeted advertising.

4. Our providers

  • Stripe: card payments. We never store your card numbers.
  • Hub2: Mobile Money payments (Orange Money, Wave…), when available.
  • Twilio: sending sign-in SMS codes.
  • Reloadly and Ding: delivering top-ups and bundles to the recipient's operator.
  • Telephony provider: routing international calls.
  • Google (Firebase): sign in with a Google account.
  • Render and MongoDB Atlas: secure hosting of the app and its database.
  • Anthropic: AI support assistant. Your support messages are sent to it to generate a reply; a human agent can take over.

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Union, with the safeguards required by the GDPR (for example the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses).

5. Retention

Account data is kept as long as the account exists. ID documents are kept only as long as needed for verification and legal obligations. Payment records are kept for accounting obligations, but detached from your personal data after the account is deleted.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct and delete your data, object to its processing or request its portability. You can:

  • edit your profile in the app (Settings);
  • delete your account from Settings → “Delete my account”, or with the account deletion form;
  • write to us at contact@allolinke.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in Italy the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

7. Security

Communications are encrypted (HTTPS), account access is protected by an SMS code or Google sign-in, and ID documents are only visible to the verification team.

8. Children

Allo Linke is not intended for people under 16.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update is shown at the top.
